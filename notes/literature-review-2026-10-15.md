([Original v2, §§II–III](https://arxiv.org/html/1509.06481v2))

Rotor results supply conditional comparisons, not an ant theorem. Bou-Rabee–Peres v1 establishes an almost-sure range/t^(2/3) limit and convex deterministic shape for iid uniform clockwise square-lattice rotors and specified extensions. An ant's relative-heading update does not establish the absolute cyclic exits needed. The checked arXiv record lists v1 and no journal reference; that metadata does not establish peer-review status. Kapri–Dhar's cone uses local-time balancing; our integral additionally assumes uniform instantaneous occupancy and coherent approximately circular compact growth. The growth exponent alone does not imply the cone. ([2026 theorem](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.23545v1); [Kapri–Dhar v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0906.5506v2))

Boundary corrections preserve the measured gap while narrowing its meaning. The same surface-site criterion does not imply the same estimator: local radii use the centroid, KD uses the origin and an ensemble mean of per-cluster variances. KD's body/caption errors disagree (±0.02/±0.002), so the manuscript reports about 0.13. The powers 2^22–2^32 are matched step counts, not matched sizes. The hole-rim split preserves the gap in a selected six-rule cohort. Wavelength follow-up is mixed, with a failed high-band prediction; persistence of low modes versus local roughness remains unresolved. Cylinder rotor work provides an independently varied lateral-size comparator, with an excursion-endpoint step clock requiring care. ([KD Eq.10/Fig.8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0906.5506v2); [cylinder §IV](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1606.01756v2); [local hull record](../experiments/archive/2026-10-hull-width/README.md))
