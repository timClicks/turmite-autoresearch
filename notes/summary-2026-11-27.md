## What was done and learned (by thread)

### Excursions Revision 5: excluded ant pilot (PR #42; Priority 1)

- Registration `78f7f9e` committed and pushed alone at 08:55 NZDT on 2026-11-25,
  before any Rev5 measurement; scripts `743beba` at 08:57.
- **P0c failed as registered** on the first attempt: the non-cohort smoke `RL@7`
  escapes the 512 smoke board, and `--check` assumed equal window counts with the
  probe. Amendment A1 (`b1a0713`, before any pilot data) moved the smoke to the
  8192 pilot board. This overrides the registered "P0 fails → stop" rule; the PR
  says plainly that a strict reviewer may treat the pilot as void.
- Pilot ran once (results `2a5966a`). Readings: RLLR UNREAD (Score_tw
  inconclusive, 0.0775 vs 0.0679; β from 1 window); **RLLRL AREA-FILLING**
  (Score_tw D, 0.0911 vs 0.0419; β = 1.932); RLLLRR UNREAD (0/36 windows with
  n_eff ≥ 30); RLLLLRLL UNREAD (1/36). Two or more UNREAD → **uninformative**.
  Reserved rules and seeds untouched; nothing retuned.
- **What was learned (post hoc, labelled as such):** the instrument cannot read
  most ants because the readout filters bind. Unread rules have per-window
  n_eff about 10–34 against a floor of 30, and the longest excursion takes 7–17%
  of the window. Longest τ / window is the only clean separator from every
  calibrator walker (≤ 0.03; restart-free IDLA, `idlar`, 0.0044).
- **Corrected twice in review.** Round 1 (2026-11-26) showed that "excursions as
  long as the territory" and "empty mid-depth bins" are not ant-specific (Eden's
  median longest τ is 1.02 A). Round 2 (2026-11-27) showed the IDLA comparison
  was itself a restart artefact. With `idlar`, the d/L share falls monotonically,
  so no restart-free walker shows the ants' weak [L, 2L) upturn (count share
  0.003–0.004). Whether that upturn is real is open in both directions; wording
  stays "heavy-tailed, possibly bimodal". n_eff separates less cleanly than first
  claimed (idlar median 50.4 vs RLLR 34).
- Instrument fix: `boundary_excursions --check` now compares only completed
  windows on escaping runs (`d73616d`, new test `tests/boundary_excursions_escape.rs`;
  the reviewer confirmed it fails on the old code). Pilot and Rev3/Rev4
  calibration outputs reproduce byte for byte in the reviewer's independent
  reruns (rustc 1.97.0, 2 threads; the author used 1.100.0 and 4 threads).
- CI green on head `18937bf`. The round-2 fixes have not been re-reviewed yet.
