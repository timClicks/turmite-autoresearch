## What was done and learned (by thread)

### Survivor slow growers (PR #37; registration `8fae18c`, A1 `b1014be`)

- P1 held: class persistence across (1e9,1e10] was 143/143 slow and 558/559
  growing. P2 held: 0/28 genuine late highways were slow in the decade before
  onset (95% upper bound about 1% per decade).
- **P3 failed:** 7/8 sampled slow rules stayed within 5% from 1e11 to 2e11;
  `RLRLRRRR` grew 8.6%. None escaped, none approached cube-root growth (1.26).
  The slow class is slow, not shown bounded.
- Amendment A1 (owner-approved in chat): constant boxes refuted for 143/143;
  the aggregate rejects a zero-offset power law (p < 0.0005) but not a log law
  (p = 0.19); 135/143 rules are individually ambiguous.
- Review 2026-11-12 (no blocking issues): "power rejected" is not the same as
  "log supported", because concave or saturating shapes also beat a convex
  zero-offset power law, and the largest single contributor looks saturated.
  The robust result is constant per-decade increments. Also: P2 passes at the
  registered sigma but would fail at a better-calibrated one, and the
  registration's "written after" statement is not accurate (see process lesson).
  These wording fixes are not yet made.

### Leak handedness cohort (PR #38; registration `19e4aa0`)

- Gates G1 and G2 passed. **P1 unresolved:** median lag +0.023, 95%
  [−0.158, +0.186]; per-rule lags spread with sd 0.38 against ~0.05 seed noise.
  Handedness matters a lot rule by rule, but the sign varies and cancels in the
  median. The calibration assumed one shared lag, which is a design defect.
- **P2 failed:** 9/36 porous in the escaping cohort (needed ≥ 50%); the collapse is all-or-nothing in 3 of 9 rules.
- Post hoc only: the sign tracked R/L composition (Spearman +0.40). The review
  confirmed the spread is real (split-half r = 0.93) and asked for that check
  to be added to `posthoc.py`; also a 6.3 MB `probe.csv` that AGENTS.md would
  put in `dumps/`, and an unpinned PEP 723 header. Not yet addressed.

### Leak minority turn (PR #39; registration `fc60f7b`)

- The registered test of #38's post hoc idea on 110 new rules. Gates passed.
  **P1, P2 and P3 failed:** Spearman(Λ, R fraction) = −0.114, 95%
  [−0.329, +0.108]; sign agreement 29/62. Calibration says this is a controlled
  negative, not low power. New dead-end signature
  `NR-20261113-leak-handedness-composition-sign` (on the PR branch).
- The per-rule spread itself replicated (sd 0.381 vs 0.383). So: the handedness
  of the forced turn changes leak tolerance strongly, and R/L composition does
  not say which way. A sign predictor, if any, has to come from the dynamics.

## Process lesson (recurring)

The reviewer found the same defect on #37 and #38: registrations say the run
and analysis scripts were "written after" the registration, but the commit
times (22–85 s apart) show they were drafted before and committed after. The
scoring is unaffected because the statistics and thresholds were frozen in the
README, but the statements are false as written. From now on, registrations
should say "drafted before, committed after; not run on data before
registration", and `run.sh` should record `sha256sum "$0"` and
`git status --porcelain` (including untracked files in the experiment folder)
in `provenance.txt`.
