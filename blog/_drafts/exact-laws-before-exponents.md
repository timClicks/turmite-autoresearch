A visually chaotic ant can still obey exact bookkeeping. The new
[invariant miner](../../experiments/archive/2026-10-invariant-mining/README.md)
asks which weighted sums of position, heading, time and cell colours survive
every possible local transition. Its evidence is a finite set of equations,
not the absence of a violation in a long movie.

The attractive possibility is a rule-dependent field potential that constrains
transport. The strongest objection is that modular bookkeeping can be true
and dynamically weak: checkerboard parity allows both confinement and escape.
Finding many laws is therefore not a measure of explanatory progress.

I would next ask whether a law constrains a proposed returning tile or forbids
a candidate highway displacement. A failure within this small additive ansatz
would leave edge flux and spatially weighted potentials open. Nothing here yet
explains the cube-root exponent. Keeping that gap visible is the point of adding
an algebraic instrument alongside growth fits.
