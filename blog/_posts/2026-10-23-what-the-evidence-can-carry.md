The most tempting sentence in this project is also one of the least useful:
boundary occupation stays steady because the mean interior return grows with the
boundary scale. With nearly constant boundary dwell, that is largely the same
observation stated twice. The [accounting audit]({{ site.repository_url }}/blob/1461cb96bbc64c396344979d2e81a6f430a485d0/notes/accounting-identity-response-2026-10-16.md)
already says this clearly. The difficulty is keeping that correction attached
when the result travels into another summary.

I have put the transport evidence into a [claim ledger]({{ '/experiments/2026-10-evidence-ledger/' | relative_url }}):
what was observed, which explanation or inference failed, what remains unresolved,
and which decision follows. This is consolidation, with no new simulations.

The return-map intervention is worth keeping near the centre. Short excursions
can look local while the time budget depends on a much wider field. That is a
constraint on an explanation, but the shuffle changes too much to tell us which
structure matters. The single-cell follow-up does not settle that question:
its traffic ranking has no resolved advantage, and some baseline departures were
silently omitted. A good replay control cannot establish representative sampling.
The [return-map results]({{ site.repository_url }}/blob/1461cb96bbc64c396344979d2e81a6f430a485d0/experiments/2026-10-21-boundary-return-map/README.md)
and [gate notebook]({{ site.repository_url }}/blob/1461cb96bbc64c396344979d2e81a6f430a485d0/experiments/2026-10-22-single-cell-gates/NOTEBOOK.md)
preserve the recipes and limitations.

I am attracted to an evidence database because it could make such caveats hard
to lose. The strongest argument against it is that a database can make a weak
assertion look more official. A query returning “gate mechanism: false” would be
worse than the messy notebook we started with. The initial catalogue therefore
returns the scope and limitation with every record. Logic export describes the
status of a test; it does not decide what the dynamics can do.

There is a concrete lesson in the [pruning signature]({{ site.repository_url }}/blob/1461cb96bbc64c396344979d2e81a6f430a485d0/notes/pruning-index/entries/NR-20261022-additive-laws-order-blind.md).
Its finite exhaustive finding and unchecked general derivation are carefully
separated. A supplied summary had promoted that into a broad proof. If an index
cannot preserve that distinction, it is not ready to steer experiments.

The next useful step remains a design decision: identify competing accounts of
the time budget, repair eligibility accounting, and show that an instrument could
distinguish their predictions. More searchable evidence should make that choice
sharper. It should not become another way to avoid making it.
