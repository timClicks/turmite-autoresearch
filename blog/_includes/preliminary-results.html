08 / Failed explanationsNegative results

{{ r.titles[7] }}

Independent turns, site cookies and edge reinforcement fail to reproduce both ant-like growth and boundary occupation in the tested settings.

How far does the exclusion go?

Only these tested settings are constrained. Memory can suppress exploration without yielding stable ant-like occupation. Short windows, escapes and seed variability prevent ruling out every stochastic explanation. The image shows illustrative trajectories, not ensemble exponents.
